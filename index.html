Flashy Flashcards Friends

Privacy policy and support. Effective October 16, 2026.

Privacy policy

Flashy Flashcards Friends is a learning app for young children. We built it so that it collects nothing.

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information, from children or from adults.
  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking tools, and no sign-in.
  • No internet use. The app works fully offline and does not send anything over the network.
  • On-device settings only. A child's first name (optional), parent settings and the star count are saved only on the device, in the app's own storage. They never leave the device and are removed when the app is deleted.
  • Read-aloud. Spoken words use Apple's built-in text-to-speech on the device.
  • No purchases or outside links. There are no in-app purchases and no links that leave the app.

Because no personal information is collected, the app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's Kids category rules.

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before releasing a new version.
